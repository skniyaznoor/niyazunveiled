title: "A Night More To Dream"
date: "Thursday, September 3, 2020"
category: "Poem"
excerpt: "Start the day by kicking bed, Dress and all, eat my bread..."
---

Start the day by kicking bed🥳,  
Dress and all, eat my bread🍞.

My cycle broke🚲 out in the middle lane,  
That's why I arrived to the College late😪.

Standing out of the classroom😓,  
See a girl and wanted to be her groom😍.

Follow her up and down as the wind😘,  
Finally get a chance to make her mine😉.

She is sitting on the bench, seems sad.  
Thought it's time to make her glad🥰.

Request and sat side to her,  
After sometime I break the peace🤞.

Asking her, what's the trouble is?  
She replied, what's your problem is?

Stand up and run as fast I can  
Feels like heart popped out from my chest.💕

Finish my class, I wander her,  
Exhaust and sat on that bench whisper.

The girl came back and sat near,  
Heart beats fast and says don't fear💖.

She start the conversation by sorry,  
I quickly reply don't worry😉.

We spent sometime together,  
Then she said thanks having you alot❤️💕.

Drop you home, if you like.  
She said, home is near hill sight💞.

In the lonely, quite way to home.  
Said all the feelings of my own💖💕.

She turn and came near.  
The alarm rang, I slam on the floor.

Wake up with the hand on forehead🤦,  
Smile and sad with the red head.💕
