**Love** ❤️💕 .... It's a tiny four letters word, but it takes billions and billions of words to prove its existence. And sometimes, it takes an eternity to be believed. Well! It's not as complicated as we make it. It's the most beautifully amazingly gorgeous feeling in this world.

Love is the only thing in this world that remains unchanged, untouched, unaffected no matter how hard you get slammed. It's like the breeze which tickles the flowers. It is the blue sky of summer that drives us forward to accept all the challenges. It's the first raindrop that touches the earth. It's the unbreakable thread that connects two hearts. It is an undying and unflinching faith that lasts for infinity to be held till eternity. It is the smile on your face and it is YOU ❤️💞 ....

Sometimes … No! Not sometimes. It always happens in everyone’s life. It may happen several times but never the same as the first time. Well! It happens with me or I say, it happens with us. And we still got it; Okay! I’m not sure about that. Are we?

Three years ago I met a girl, very beautiful and pretty and charming as well. And if you look at her for a few seconds, I can assure you that your heart will fill with joy & happiness. I don’t know, how did she have that magic? But it’s very effective. Well! I don’t believe in the concept of love at first sight. But to be honest, I fell in love with her. Yeah! I know, it sounds odd, meeting a girl for the first time and falling in love with her. But how can’t I fall in love? The way she was tucking her hair back to her ears; that smile of her could bring a dead person to live. Those drugged & deep black eyes have the power to sink the entire ocean. And that cute & little nose, which tickles the wind. Those ground-touching long & shiny hair is taking my breath away. Well! I can go on & on … 

Bani ... It's not just a name, it's a world of mine. It still gives me Goosebumps, whenever I remember that day. The day, when I hold her hands for the very first time, I was so nervous and scared. You have no idea, how was I collecting myself? Those worm hands of her extracted a beat of my heart which never returned to me. And I don't know how? But I went on my knees and proposed to her. Can you imagine? I never hold her hand till that day when I proposed. And that day is still a miracle for me. 

And I still remember, after a month, we were walking back towards her home with hand in hand. And we were about to reach her home, then she stopped and hugged me tightly and slowly whispered in my ears, "I felt very safe when I was in your arms", " Your first glance and my heart skipped its beat, and that beat became yours forever", "You are the best gift given to me by God", "I love you more than anything", "Please! Never leave me". I ran out of words at that time, tears rolled out from my eyes. It felt like God has given me everything. And all of a sudden I became the luckiest person on this planet.

But life doesn't go that easy. And mine wasn't that different. A thunderstorm came into my life and took my world away from me. And that thunderstorm became a permanent nightmare of mine. And I still get hunted by those memories. Well! She is fine and doing well in her life. But I'm not in her world anymore.

Well! This is it. I am Varun Shahni and this is my little love story 💖💕 ....
