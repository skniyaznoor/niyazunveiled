**M**emories are precious but they live in the past. Some memories might break us but in the end, it's the good stuff that stays. Moving forward may be difficult for some time but steps matter, it should be small but never backward. Only you need a heart that will to end the barrier. And that might be difficult to find.

"Why isn't she picking up my calls?" Nile talks to himself. And all of a sudden, a hand taps on his shoulder.

  

"Are you looking for me?" Shivani said.

  

His eyes are stunned by the glazing beauty of her, his voice choked to utter a single word, and his legs froze there as if the world stopped moving.

"What?" Shivani said.

  

"Ahh ... You look ...." He stunned.

  

"What?" She asked.

"Now, come with me. I've something to show you." Shivani dragged Nile.

"It's beautiful."

"No! I just want to show you this." She justified.

  

"Really!!" Nile chuckled.

  

"Now! Please make a wish." Shivani said.

  

"Okay! I wish to be hugged by you. Now!" Nile said.

"What? Why are you staring at me with this adorably beautiful face? I can wish for anything." Nile blushed.
