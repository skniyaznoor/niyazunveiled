**You** ❤️💕 ....

  

Did you know you live in me? Yeah! It sounds weird, how can a person live in two different places at a particular time? Well, it's beyond Heisenberg's uncertainty principle. But one thing is for sure(maybe) I got the best version of you. There is something magical in you. In case you don't know, let me tell you; you are the most beautiful person in this world. And your smile, well! that's a blessing to the whole universe. 

  

You are more necessary than the heartbeat. And heart, it's a very complicated thing. It can pump both love and pain simultaneously. It can be broken several times but it needs you to mend itself. As I have a lot of you so it can break infinite times. And there is another thing, no matter how many times it broke. It will never stop loving and trusting. It's all because of you. I can die multiple times to get a drop of you.

_(Let's go to the world where only You remains)_

  

You are that wind that gives flight to the butterflies and you are that wind that makes the ship sail. You are the happiness that comes from making others happy. You are the first sunlight that touches the ground that tortured by night. You are the first drop of rain that giggles the earth. 

  

And your eyes are from another world. It can drink the entire ocean in one sip. It can twist anyone's life. They have the power to make weak ones strong. They enlighten the world. They make the flowers blossom. They make the river flow. They exist love to be loved. 

  

Imagining a world without you means a world without love and that will never be a world, that's hell. And you are the most precious gift to this world and to me. **"You are love and love is you"**. I want you, I need you every second of my life. This world can stay crazy all I need is you and your hand in mine.
