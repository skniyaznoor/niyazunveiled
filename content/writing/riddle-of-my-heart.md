**A**s a leaf falls slowly to the earth, she falls into my life. Like the wind from the spring that blossoms the bud, she comes with silence to my colorless world. The fragrance of her still stays fresh in my dream when she makes the stone beat with her touch, which they called heart. She is like the fireflies in the darkest night which lights my universe and makes it colorful.

  

**N**ymph which holds the purity of life has blessed her to blaze. God himself creates grace in the form of her. She is the warmth of the heart, peace to the eyes, and a magical touch to the petals by the butterflies. The trace of her bare fingers is still there in my heart and the magic she left there is still beating.

**K**iss that lands on my lips twists my soul and merge in heaven. The restlessness, the misery, and all the suffering vanish in thin air. The warmth in her arms heals my cold corpse. The peace in her arms consumes me. The undying and unflinching love that she is, deforms my soul and integrates with hers.

  

  

**I**'m enchanted by her mesmerizing voice and drugged by those oceanly deep eyes. Her hypnotic eyes have the power to make an abandoned soul dance. And the word "Beautiful" is created especially to describe her. I can wait for an eternity in misery just for one glimpse of her face. And her smile is from another world and is a blessing to this universe. I can die numerously just to be the reason for her smile.

  

  

**T**hinking about her hurts my heart and it makes me fall for her even harder. How can someone be that elegant and pretty at the same time? The day then we come across the time standstill at that moment. As if the universe is beaconing that it is destined for us to be together. Like an invisible force acting on us, like a missing piece is found.

  

  



**A** letter that is left unsend holds a lot more emotions than the sent one. That dry red rose of those years in the book smells more than the fresh ones. That unerasable smile, those adorable big eyes, and that mole in the neck skip the beat then, now, and forever. And when we met at that moment in that instance this lunatic heart utters "She is Love".

  

Smile! It won't kill you. ❤️💕 ....
