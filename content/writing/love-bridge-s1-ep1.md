**Love** ❤️ ✨ ....

Well! Love, itself is a bridge that connects two hearts. It's the weirdest feeling ever. It breaches us to the limits of the unthinkable. It is so ridiculously delightful and so are the people in it. Well! It isn't that hard to describe than to be in that ineffably uncontrollable emotion. So let's begin an incredible journey of love. 

  

"Nile", a very energetic youth seeking changes in his life. Well! As they say "Change is mandatory to progress". Life has several phases and each phase prefers a different you. Well! It's valid, but here is the query, what illustrates change? 

  

Well! We all are learners and we all are struggling to reach our destinations. Some get their skies, some are striving to put their names in skies, and some skies stay nameless. And time rolls on, it's the beauty or cruse of time; that it changes every second. And with each running needle, it tells us "Life is changing".

  

And youth isn't all about love. It's about ambition and passion. Youths are flexible and vulnerable but hopeful. 

Being youth, we don't give up easily. We may fail, and fall but never steps back. Yeah! We are kind of stubborn and inexperienced too, which makes our risk appetite so high to take the chances that literally make us fearless. We have flaws, we aren't perfect but we are always ready to fly. Sometimes we act a bit stupid but that's how we learn and Nile is one of the lighting learners.

**Nile's tale.**

**14th April 2022**

  

"Nile! It's time for you to stop now. It's been freaking four years, everything comes with a price and you have paid enough for that. Please! Stop punishing yourself and let go of the rage inside you." Avantika said.

  

"Go away!" Nile said.

  

"I can't" 

"Can't you see. I can't." she shouted.

"Just look at you, what have you done to yourself. This isn't right. You are destroying yourself." She sobs.

  

Nile looked away.

"Please! Nile, listen to me." She begged.

  

"I can't stop now. I'm too close." Nile pleaded.

  

"Enough of this now." "You are coming with me." She said and hold my hand.

  

"I'm not going anywhere." Nile raged.

  

"Listen, there is a very thin line between a genius and an idiot, don't cross it. I know it has been hard for you, but trust me you are better than that. I've seen you from the beginning, you are the most energetic, optimistic, stubborn kind of boy I ever met. It's hurt to see you like this." she pitied.

  

"Like what?" Nile raged.

  

"Like a bloody broken psychopath. Look around you, is this what you call living?" She replied.

  

"It's too late to step back." Nile said.

  

"It's a bit late but it is never that late for a new start." She quoted.

  

"No! I can't." Nile replied and unhanded. 

  

"Please! For God's sake, it's becoming a maniac now, you have to quit this insanity." Avantika exhausted.

  

"You are chasing a ghost. Shivani is dead. She is dead, everybody has accepted that and moved on. But why can't you? It's been four long years." She said in anger with tears.

  

"Shivani can't die. She is alive, I just need to find her." Nile replied.

  

"Shivani is dead." Avantika raged. 

  

"She isn't." Nile raged.

  

"Shivani is dead. She doesn't need to be found. And even if she isn't, then where is she now? Why isn't she coming back to her family and friends? What's stopping her from us?" Avantika said.

  

"I don't know." Nile replied. 

  

"Because she is dead." Avantika said. 

  

"If you repeat that sentence then I rip off your throat." Nile replied. 

  

"Still it won't change anything." She said.

"So stop pushing yourself so hard. And try to understand, that we are not your enemies. And stop being an enemy of yourself." She concerned.

  

"Stop pitting on me and leave." Nile said.

  

"This stubbornness gonna cause you enough of suffering." She replied.

  

"Are you done?" Nile said.

  

"What's wrong with you?" 

"Believe it or not, you need a break. And need to get out of this dilemma before it's too late." Avantika said.

  

"It's none of your business anyway. So keep your pity to yourself." Nile replied.

  

"Everybody has a weakness and for me it's you." She replied and takes a pause.

  

"That day you lost your love, Kartik lost his sister, and I lost a friend." 

"And I don't want to lose more."

"And I'm truly sorry for not being there."

"We all lose something, but it doesn't mean to waste our lives for that."

"I love you and I always will." She confessed.
