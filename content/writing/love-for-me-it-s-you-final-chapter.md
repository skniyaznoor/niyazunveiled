You 💖 .... 

  

Well! Before you, I gonna tell a little experiment of Schrodinger. Schrodinger is a physicist, he did a thought experiment involving a cat. He imagined taking a cat and placing it in a sealed box with a device that had a 50% chance of killing the cat in the next hour. And in the end, he asked "what is the state of the cat?" Common sense suggests that the cat is either alive or dead. According to quantum physics, at the instance, before the box is opened the cat is equal parts alive and dead at the same time. It's only when the box is opened that we see a single definite state until then, the cat is a blur of the probability of one thing and another. Sounds a little complicated but it's quite simple. In other words, the cat is part alive and dead.

  

Well! This quantum concept can be visualized if you were in love. You are mixed of all the emotions from happy to sad, shocked to surprised, pain to peace at the same time before you expressed your feelings. After that, you were either a savior or murderer of your and others' feelings. If you ever had a chance to be a savior of yourself then express it because it is the best thing to do.  It's more like a selfish act but it is mandatory for you to survive.
