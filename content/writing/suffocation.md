Smile 🙂
There are uncountable stars in the galaxies. But It doesn't matter if one star gets lost in the darkness, the galaxies will still look brighter. But before losing itself in the darkness, that star gives one last hope of light to make a wish that flows in dreams. Smile is same as star.... It doesn't matter to anyone, if someone loses his smile.

Smile - Whatever it takes

People say "Smile is the best medicine in all situations." But they don't say, "how to bring a smile?" Well! It's not that easy to smile. Smile is one of the most envied thing in this world. And there is a funny and weird relation between smile and pain, these two are two opposite sides of a coin but dwell in one place. 

Well! What's pain? Pain is a thing which lives in a broken person to pump the dying heart. And that's give  rise a smile, a very dangerous smile which doesn't have any fear to get lost. It sticks with him forever. 

Did you ever try to find that smile which used to come from your heart? No. If yes, then did you get it. And even if you get that, you can't fix yourself. Because it's like childhood, once it is gone then it won't come back. One thing you can do is leave. Leave it and move on. Because you get only one life, Make it useful and worth living.

For how long will you spend your life in darkness,?light has to know that from where the fire is born. Yeah! There are broken dreams floating in dark but they haven't fade yet. So smile, not to show others but to choose yourself over others.

"If this smile starts to hurt you then you can know  that I'm not in your reach anymore."
